A considered approach
to ownership

Established in Doha, TM Holding LLC is a private limited liability company incorporated in the Qatar Financial Centre.

Our focus is the acquisition, ownership, management and leasing of civil aircraft and maritime vessels. A long-term perspective guides the way we consider each asset, its purpose and its lifecycle.

Our approach separates ownership and oversight from day-to-day operation. Operational responsibilities are intended to sit with appropriately qualified operators and specialist service providers under asset-specific agreements.

CORPORATE INFORMATION

A clear corporate foundation

Information for manufacturers, counterparties and professional advisers.

Registered in Qatar

TM Holding LLC is recorded as a registered QFC LLC, with licence status shown as “Licensed” in the supplied company registration record.

Responsible asset oversight

Our intended oversight model brings together technical, financial and contractual review: the condition of each asset, service-provider responsibilities, budgets, insurance and expenditure. Its scope is defined for each acquisition and operating arrangement.

Civil asset ownership

The stated purpose of proposed acquisitions is civil use through ownership, management and leasing. End-use declarations and due diligence documentation are provided for each transaction separately.
