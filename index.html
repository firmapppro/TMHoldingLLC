TM HOLDING LLC

Private by nature.
Professional in approach.

TM Holding LLC is a private holding company established in Doha, Qatar in 2026, focused on the acquisition, ownership, management and leasing of aviation and maritime assets.

Our purpose is to provide a structured foundation for ownership: clear responsibilities, informed decisions and coordinated oversight throughout the life of each asset.

We bring together financial, technical and contractual expertise to support responsible ownership, preserve asset condition and manage the commitments that accompany it.

Our relationships are selective and developed through trusted introductions and direct engagement. Discretion and continuity guide the way we work.

AVIATION & MARITIME

Distinct assets.
A consistent standard of care.

Two asset classes. One disciplined perspective.

01 / AVIATION

Aviation assets

Aircraft ownership involves decisions that extend well beyond acquisition. We oversee ownership structures, operator arrangements, maintenance planning, insurance, budgets and lifecycle costs.

Operational execution is entrusted to appropriately qualified operators and specialist providers under clearly defined agreements.

02 / MARITIME

Maritime assets

Vessel ownership requires continuous attention to technical condition, operating arrangements and long-term expenditure. We coordinate oversight of management agreements, maintenance and refit planning, insurance, budgets and contractual commitments.

Day-to-day operations are entrusted to appropriately qualified managers, crews and specialist providers, with defined responsibilities and reporting arrangements.

OUR APPROACH

Value throughout ownership.

We consider each asset in the context of its intended use, obligations and long-term ownership requirements.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Evaluate purpose, technical condition, financial commitments and transaction risks.

  2. 02

    Structuring

    Establish clear ownership, contractual arrangements and decision-making responsibilities.

  3. 03

    Acquisition

    Coordinate due diligence, specialist advice and a documented acquisition process.

  4. 04

    Oversight

    Review technical reports, budgets, expenditure and the performance of appointed providers.

  5. 05

    Continuity

    Plan maintenance, renewal and eventual disposal with a clear view of the asset’s lifecycle.

GOVERNANCE

Reliability through discipline.

Our approach to ownership rests on clear accountability, professional expertise and consistent oversight.

01

Clear responsibilities

Defined authority, documented decisions and clear responsibilities across owners, operators, managers and advisers.

02

Financial control

Structured budgeting, expenditure review and reporting that supports informed decisions and accountability.

03

Professional oversight

Coordination of qualified specialists, review of technical and contractual obligations, and attention to the condition and continuity of each asset.

04

Discretion

Careful handling of corporate and asset information, with disclosure limited to the needs of authorised relationships and specific transactions.
