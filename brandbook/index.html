03 / THE MARKTM HOLDING LLC / IDENTITY GUIDELINES

One monogram.
No ornament.

A geometric TM built around a shared vertical axis.

The horizontal T establishes a horizon. The open, angular M gives the mark a stable base and an upward rhythm.

These are visual associations, rather than literal aircraft or vessel symbols. Removing the previous cartouche, metallic facets and fine ornaments improves clarity at small sizes.

The mark must remain a flat vector. Do not add a crest, stars, shine, bevels or 3D effects.

03 / THE MARKTM HOLDING LLC / IDENTITY GUIDELINES

Space to breathe.

x

Clear space = one-sixth of the mark height.

Use the 120-unit SVG artboard as the reference. The visible mark is 87 units high; minimum clear space is therefore 14.5 units around its visible bounds.

For a complete signature, measure x using the monogram. Keep neighbouring text, trim edges and other graphics outside this area.

Preferred space

Use 2x or more for covers and prominent brand moments. Minimum clear space is a limit, not a target.

04 / COLOURTM HOLDING LLC / IDENTITY GUIDELINES

Balance & contrast.

IVORY 60%   /   FOREST 30%   /   STONE 8%   /   BRASS 2%

Space carries the identity. Colour supports it.

These proportions are an editorial starting point, not a formula for every page. Use forest for primary content, ivory for breathing room and stone for quiet supporting fields.

Accessible combinations

Forest on ivory: approximately 11:1.
Ivory on forest: approximately 11:1.
Slate on ivory: approximately 4.7:1.

Brass is decorative; do not use it for small text on ivory. All controls require visible focus and enough contrast. Colour must never be the only way to communicate status.

06 / ART DIRECTIONTM HOLDING LLC / IDENTITY GUIDELINES

A long-term horizon.

Quiet scale. Natural light. An uncluttered frame.

Use editorial imagery with muted sea, stone and architectural tones. Leave space for the subject and for the page itself. Images should be still and purposeful.

Exclude

Stock handshakes, glowing globes, dramatic gold sunsets, particle effects, simulated live telemetry and generic luxury montages.

Obtain usage rights for any future photography. Caption proposed or illustrative assets accurately. Never present an image as proof of ownership.
